# PRIVACY POLICY for LINKUP

**Effective Date: May 14, 2026**

At LINKUP, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our founder community. This policy outlines how we handle your data.

### 1. DATA WE COLLECT
- **Identity Information:** Name, email, bio, and profile photos.
- **Verification Data:** "Proof of Life" photos used strictly for network integrity.
- **Usage Data:** Device information, push tokens, and interaction logs.
- **Content:** Posts, messages, and comments shared on the platform.

### 2. HOW WE USE YOUR DATA
- To provide and personalize the LINKUP experience.
- To verify the identity of founders and prevent fraud.
- To send important notifications and alerts.
- To improve our matchmaking algorithms.

### 3. DATA SHARING
- We **NEVER** sell your personal data to third parties.
- Your profile data is visible to other authenticated members of the LINKUP network.
- We may share data with service providers (like Firebase or AI services) to facilitate the app's functionality.

### 4. YOUR RIGHTS (GDPR & CCPA)
- You have the right to access, correct, or delete your data at any time.
- You can delete your account permanently through the Profile settings.
- You can opt-out of push notifications through your device settings.

### 5. SECURITY
We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your data from unauthorized access.

### 6. CONTACT
For any privacy concerns, contact us at privacy@linkup.pro.
