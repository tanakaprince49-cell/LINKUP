# LINKUP 16.10.3 — Linky Explains WHY, LvlUp's Standards (2026-10-27)

Rebuilt the `/app/lvlup` matching around LvlUp Ventures' actual published
standards (studied at lvlup.vc + First Check Fund page), and every pick now
explains WHY it surfaced. Hero regains the wordmark beside the logos.

## What LvlUp wants (from their site — our matching now speaks it)
- 25,000+ startup applications/year; ~900–1,100 First Check apps/month.
  Their pain is filtering at volume — the demo's pitch.
- Applicant mix: AI 54%, B2B, SaaS, Consumer, FinTech, HealthTech, DeepTech.
- First Check: $1K–$10K at idea/pre-formation, deck + conversation, standard
  SAFEs — "conviction before traction"; follow-on $250K seed → $5M.
- Founder screens: learning velocity, founder focus ("one tight sentence"),
  grit/ownership, coachability; distribution over product alone.
- Africa is their #3 applicant source (473 apps): "Talent is global and
  early capital still is not."

## Server (`api/publicSeo.js`, deterministic — still zero AI jitter)
- `lvlupScore` v2: thesis coverage (×68) + 7 LvlUp-weighted founder signals
  (×4): shipped, repeat shipper, one-sentence bio ≤140 chars, 3+ stack,
  seeking intros, verified, founder title. No-term baseline 56 + 5/signal.
- NEW `lvlupWhy`: per-pick receipts — Thesis fit (which terms, WHICH FIELDS:
  skills/projects/bio/role/seeking), Shipping velocity, Founder focus (quotes
  the one-liner), Technical depth, Open to intros, Verified identity. Max 5.
- NEW `lvlupLane`: 0 shipped projects → "First Check" (idea-to-demo lane);
  1+ → "Seed track" (seed-lane signals). `brief` format untouched.
