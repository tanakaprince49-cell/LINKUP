Account and data deletion request

LINKUP users can request deletion of their account and associated app data at any time. Use this page if you cannot access the in-app delete account option.

Email deletion request Contact support

How to request deletion

  1. Email linkupp49@gmail.com from the same email address used for your LINKUP account.
  2. Use the subject LINKUP account and data deletion request.
  3. Include your LINKUP account email and, if you know it, your LINKUP user ID.
  4. We will verify ownership before deleting account data.

What gets deleted

  • Your LINKUP profile and public profile listing.
  • Profile photos, gallery photos, saved profiles, profile analytics, notifications, and app preferences.
  • Conversation records, matches, opportunity activity, and other user-generated app data associated with your account where deletion is technically possible.
  • Your Firebase Authentication account, when ownership is verified and no security re-authentication step is required from you.

Timing and retained data

We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days. Some limited records may be retained if required for legal, security, fraud prevention, billing, or dispute purposes, then deleted or anonymized when no longer needed.

You can also delete your account inside the app by opening your profile, opening settings, and choosing Delete Account Permanently.
