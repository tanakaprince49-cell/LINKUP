Privacy policy

LINKUP is an adults-only social and dating platform for founders and builders. This page explains how we collect, use, share, and protect your data, and it also links to our published child safety standards.

Email privacy contact Child safety standards

What we collect

Account and profile details such as name, email, username, age, bio, photos, skills, interests, startup ideas, and preferences.

Messages, reports, blocks, matches, saved profiles, notifications, and other content you choose to share in LINKUP.

Device and usage data such as app version, device type, push token, crash logs, IP address, and feature usage.

Verification, subscription, and AI feature inputs that you choose to submit through the app.

How we use it

  • To create and secure your account.
  • To show your profile to other members when you choose to be visible.
  • To support messaging, matching, search, discovery, reporting, moderation, and account safety.
  • To power notifications, analytics, subscriptions, and AI-assisted features you choose to use.
  • To respond to support, abuse, legal, or fraud-prevention requests.

How we share it

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with service providers that help operate LINKUP, with app store or payment processors, with other members when you choose to publish or message content, and with law enforcement or other authorities when required by law or needed to protect users and the platform.

Deletion and retention

You can delete your account in the app or by emailing linkupp49@gmail.com. We aim to process verified deletion requests within a reasonable period, while retaining only the minimum data needed for legal, security, anti-fraud, or billing reasons.

Security and children

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect data. LINKUP is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow children to create accounts, and if we learn that a child account or child sexual abuse/exploitation content is present, we may remove the content, disable the account, preserve evidence as needed, and escalate where required by law.
