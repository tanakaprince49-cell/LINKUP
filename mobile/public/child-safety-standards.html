Child safety standards

LINKUP is an adults-only social and dating app for founders and builders. This page explains how we prevent, detect, report, and respond to child safety risks, including child sexual abuse and exploitation.

Report a child safety issue Privacy policy

1. Age restriction

LINKUP is intended for adults 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow children to create accounts or use the service.

2. Zero tolerance for CSAE

We prohibit child sexual abuse material, child sexual exploitation, grooming, sextortion, trafficking, or any sexualized content involving minors. Content or behavior that violates these standards is not allowed on LINKUP.

3. Reporting and moderation

Users can report profiles, messages, photos, and other content through the in-app report and block tools. Reports are reviewed by the LINKUP team or other safety processes we use to keep the platform safe.

  • We may restrict or remove reported content and accounts.
  • We may limit messaging, discovery, or account access while a report is reviewed.
  • We may preserve evidence or records as needed for safety, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

4. Escalation

If we identify child sexual abuse or exploitation content, or receive a credible report of such content, we may disable the account, remove the content, preserve relevant records, and escalate to the appropriate authorities or reporting body when required by law.

5. User contact and safety point of contact

Child safety contact for LINKUP: linkupp49@gmail.com

This address can be used for child safety reports, privacy questions, and account deletion requests.

6. Policy scope

This published standard applies to LINKUP, our social and dating app, and to the community spaces we operate for founders and builders.
