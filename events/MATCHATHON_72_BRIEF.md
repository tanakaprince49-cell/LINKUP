## ⚡ The Concept
**Matchathon 72** is a 72-hour online hackathon with a twist: **nobody enters with a team.** Every participant joins solo, builds a LINKUP profile, and LINKUP's AI matching engine pairs builders with complementary founders, designers and marketers into balanced squads. Teams then have 72 hours to ship a working MVP and demo it live.

It's not just a hackathon — it's a live demonstration of LINKUP's core product at scale: *matching the right builders with the right ideas, instantly.*

## 📅 Format & Run of Show

**Platform:** Discord (community HQ) + LINKUP app (matching + profiles) + Zoom (opening, workshops, finals)

**Week 0 — Registration**
- Participants sign up via LINKUP profile (skills, interests, looking-for = match data)
- Solo entry only — the app forms the teams

**Day 1 (Fri) — Kickoff & Matching 🔀**
- Opening ceremony (30 min) — welcome from LINKUP + LVL UP keynote: "What we look for in founding teams"
- **The Great Matching:** AI proposes teams of 3–4 (founder/dev/designer mix), teams confirm in-app
- Team channels open in Discord

**Day 1–2 (Fri–Sun night) — Build 🛠️**
- Optional workshops: idea validation, no-code speedruns, pitching 101
- Mentor office hours (LVL UP partners + ecosystem operators)
- Teams post build-logs to LINKUP's Forge feed (public progress = marketing fuel)

**Day 3 (Sun/Mon) — Finals 🎤**
- Top 10 teams demo: 3-minute pitch + 2-min Q&A
- Judges: LVL UP partners + guest founders
- Winners announced live; all demos clipped for socials
